Mr. Park

Mr. Park is a beginner photographer based in Perth, Western Australia. This project documents public parks around the Perth area as part of a learning journey in photography and photo editing.

The goal of this project is to build a visual record of local parks and contribute original photographs to platforms such as Google Maps.
