About Mr. Park

Mr. Park is a beginner photographer based in Perth, Western Australia. This project was created to learn photography and photo editing by documenting public parks around the Perth area.

The focus of this project is on landscapes, park features, and public green spaces. All photographs published on this website are original and taken by Mr. Park.

Some photographs may also be contributed to platforms such as Google Maps to help improve publicly available information about parks.

This is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with any local council or government authority.
